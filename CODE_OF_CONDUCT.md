# Code of Conduct

LaunchDesk is for people trying to learn, build, and improve their practical options. Be direct, useful, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Help people understand the tradeoffs.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions practical and evidence-based.
- Welcome beginners who are willing to learn.
- Respect different backgrounds, budgets, and goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Spam, scams, or deceptive monetization advice.
- Pressuring people into risky financial decisions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who harm the project or community. If you see a problem, open a private security/contact channel with the maintainer when available.
